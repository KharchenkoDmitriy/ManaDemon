# Party A — rebuttal to Party B

## 1. WHERE B IS RIGHT AND I CONCEDE

- **Cast commitment (C15).** A cast in flight cannot be re-decided; the design's re-decide-at-every-event loop gives the sim a free option the human never has, and it biases overheal down on exactly the direct heals triage depends on. I missed this entirely; it is the single best mechanical catch in either paper.
- **Causality as an explicit invariant (C12-1).** B states it harder than I did and is right that it is most of the gap, not a correction to it: without `t' <= t_decision`, `early`/`overheal`/`idle` are charges for not being clairvoyant.
- **HP-at-cast as the marginal recorded field (C9-3, C10, C16).** "Rejuvenation R12 on Trecoda at 87%, 296 mana, 45% wasted" is unarguable where a 45% family fraction is arguable. One field, huge evidentiary return, and it makes every label falsifiable at the event level.
- **Ship a habit line before the simulator (C16).** My v0.7.1 "where the mana went" card needs the damage recorder; B's needs one field on `SPELL_CAST_SUCCESS`. B's is strictly cheaper and lands sooner. Order: B's line first, my physical floor second.
- **Utility and form mana are not a smooth drip (Missing #1).** 10,014 mana / 12.6% of the run, 26% of the hard pull, arriving as 445-mana lumps in combat. `utilityMp5 = 0` is not a simplification, it is a broken mana identity. I raised form; B is right that utility is the bigger and simpler half.
- **Mana tolerance should be tighter than mine, HP looser (C8).** 1%/3% mana and 5%/15% HP is better calibrated than my 2%/5% and 3%/10%: the mana curve is an accounting identity, HP carries pets, absorbs and range.
- **Record `RM.apiBase`/`RM.apiCasting` per mana sample (C8).** The in-combat drink buff at +2212.81 (+486/tick to +2215.64) would fail validation for a reason with nothing to do with the model. Two numbers per sample. Excellent catch.
- **Split the validation gate (C8).** Mana failure kills everything; one target's HP failure excludes that target from the diff. My all-or-nothing gate was too brittle and would have made "most fights fail" self-fulfilling.
- **Report `max(waitRun)`, not only `waitFraction` (C7).** One variable, and it is the variable that distinguishes an unhurried human from a machine reading the future.
- **Per-role damage aggregation buries the structure (C13).** One target took 1,200 of 1,979 heal events (61%); a "mean DAMAGER rate" describes nobody. Per-target windows tagged by role, not pooled by role.
- **Triage rules must be sized on the non-crit heal (C1).** `RankMath:EventPrediction`'s `direct` already strips crit; sizing a floor rule on `heal x (1 + 0.5*crit)` is short exactly when it matters. Concede without reservation.
- **Compliance evidence is decisive against rebinding advice (C14).** 73–100% max-rank casts against a dashboard that has suggested a downrank for versions, plus 0-of-4 potion alerts, is a run experiment. My "one bind change per report under five gates" was a slower road to B's answer; route it to the new-rank moment instead.
- **Loop closure (Missing #8).** "Since your last card: overheal 39% -> 31%, mana/pull -0.8k." Nothing else in either paper measures whether the feature works. Ship it with the first card, not after.
- **Rules 3 and 4 overlap (C2).** `roll` and `hot below=0.80` both put a HoT on someone; the grid contains behaviourally identical plans. Real waste in the search space, independent of how large the family should be.
- **C5 is low-stakes (C5).** I overweighted it. The scheduler ordering bugs B names (refresh cancelling a scheduled tick, expiry vs bloom, death between tick and cast) are the actual cost, and they are unit-testable only in-game.
- **`prehot` as a label (C10).** Regrowth ticking `243 (243 overheal)` six times before the pull at +2153 is spent mana in no fight bracket at all. My label set missed it too.

## 2. WHERE B IS WRONG

**B: "MC's only real product is P(death), and you cannot buy it at a sane price: 200 samples x 400 plans is an hour."**
That is not the proposal and the arithmetic is against a strawman. My C1 is K≈30 replicates of the **3 reported plans**, after the search: 90 runs, not 80,000. At B's own 10–20 ms that is **0.9–1.8 s, once, off the critical path**. B then substitutes an empirical ensemble that answers a different question: replicates measure outcome variance *within* a scenario (does this plan survive its own crit rolls), the ensemble measures variance *across* scenarios (does this plan survive a different pull). Both are wanted; neither substitutes. And B's ensemble is undermined by B's own C9: retaining "the 8 most expensive fights per zone by mana" selects on the outcome variable, so "held in 16 of your last 18 pulls" is computed on a sample deliberately biased toward hard pulls. Value-based retention and empirical robustness cannot both be right as stated.

**B: "Fixed order, and shrink it further — 3 spells, 2 thresholds, 1 roll target... Meanwhile the family is missing NS+HT and shapeshifting. A family that can't express NS+HT can't reproduce the fight it is coaching."**
These two sentences want opposite things in the same paragraph. Reproduction fidelity (replay must express NS+HT, ToL shifts, utility) is a property of the **simulator**, and I agree it is mandatory; expressiveness of the **plan family** is a property of the search, and shrinking it to 3 spells guarantees the replay of the hard pull cannot be reproduced by any plan in the family. The resolution is that replay and planning use the same engine but different action sets: the engine must model everything the log contains (NS+HT once, 20 ToL lines, 6 MotW, 4 Thorns — verified by grep), the plan family stays small. Without separating them, B's C8 validation gate can never pass on the one fight with structure.

**B: "Mana is the wrong headline currency — convert it to seconds of downtime."**
The insight ("you had 2.3k of headroom, nothing needed to change") is right and I adopt it; the conversion as the headline is wrong. `Engine/PullBudget.lua` computes `afford = math.floor(mana / perPull)` from a **median over at most `RECENT = 5` same-zone fights** (and falls back to "recent fights anywhere" below two). It is a floor function on a 5-sample median: a 2.3k saving buys one extra pull only if it happens to cross an integer boundary, and the same saving reads as 0 or 1 extra pulls depending on which five trash pulls are in the window. Mana is the primitive the model actually computes and calibrates; downtime is a derived, discretised, small-sample line that belongs **under** it. Keep mana as the number, add B's verdict line, and print the conversion only when `n >= 4` in-zone.

**B: "Use quantiles... let the preset be 'tank at p90, everyone else at p50'."**
A vector of per-target marginal quantiles is not a scenario. If tank damage is at its p90 and four others sit at their p50, the joint event has probability well under 10% and, worse, an undefined one — the marginals are correlated (the same pull that spikes the tank spikes the group). B's own C13 risk line admits the consequence ("every synthetic scenario harsher than a typical pull") without noticing the cause. The correct object is a *joint* sample: replay an actual recorded fight at a chosen severity rank, or define the big-hit process in units of the target's own maxHP (my C13) and let the scenario generate the joint pattern. Marginal quantiles are fine as a *description* of a corpus and wrong as a *generator*.

**B: "Reaction in the plan as a parameter — default 0.5s, not 0.3s."**
Right about the number, wrong about where it applies. The log settles it: inter-cast gaps have **p10 = 1.50s, p25 = 1.51s** — when chaining, the author lands on the GCD exactly, because the client queues the next cast. A blanket 0.5s makes every instant cost 2.0s instead of 1.5s, i.e. **25% fewer casts per unit time** (worse than the 17% I costed at 0.3s), which makes every plan look cheaper and safer than it is and biases the coaching gap in precisely the direction B spends C12 arguing against. Reaction applies **only at decision points after idling**, where the human must notice; between chained casts it is zero. B's 0.5s is the right default for that restricted case.

**B: "Raise the gate to >=25s and >=8 own casts."**
This raises the gate on the same corpus in which the excluded short pulls carry overheal of 28%, 30%, 31% — at or above the run's average. Raising it makes the **habits** statistics strictly more biased: they would be computed on systematically cleaner fights than the ones being played, and the headline finding (29% of mana into full-health targets) is drawn from precisely the pulls B wants to discard. B's argument is sound for a different tier: fights worth *planning* on do need structure. So the gate is not one number — full event streams gate high (B is right), summary/habit rows gate low at >=15s and >=4 casts (25 of 30 qualify; I am right). One gate cannot serve both.

**B: "If, after two weeks of that line in his chat frame, the author's overheal has not moved, then the planner will not move it either, because a rotation card is the same advice with more machinery."**
The conditional does not hold. The habit line reports a *fraction*; the planner reports a *counterfactual with a price* ("this pull, plan B costs 3.9k and nobody drops below 42%"). The 0-for-4 potion alerts and the ignored downrank suggestion are both evidence against *unpriced advice at decision moments* — DECISIONS v0.6 §9 diagnoses exactly that — and the planner is the first thing in the project that prices anything. Ship the line first, absolutely; but conditioning six components on a two-week behavioural null test with n=1 subject is a decision rule that will kill the feature on noise.

**B (omission): no falsifier for the search, and no residual for the classifier.**
B's paper has no lower bound, so "best plan 3.9k" remains a minimum over one parameterisation with nothing to distinguish "the family is near-optimal" from "the search is stuck in a local optimum". `Sum_i max(0, D_i - (hp0_i - floor_i*maxHP_i)) / maxHPM` with a `casts x 1.5s <= duration` check costs one O(events) pass, and any plan scoring below it is a bug. Likewise B adds three labels (`utility`, `prehot`, `shift`) but still has no `unclassified` bucket and no `sum(labels) + utility + shifts = fight spend` identity — the check that would have caught the missing 10,014 mana in the first place. A taxonomy whose parts do not sum to the whole reports percentages of an unknown denominator.

## 3. SYNTHESIS I CAN LIVE WITH

- **C1.** Deterministic EV for search and ranking; triage rules sized on the non-crit heal (B); ~30 crit/jitter replicates of the 3 reported plans for `P(floor violation)` (~1 s, off the critical path); cross-recording replay as a *separate*, later robustness line, on an unbiased fight sample.
- **C2.** Small fixed-order family for the *planner* (B's shrink, minus the duplicate `roll`/`hot` overlap), full action coverage in the *engine* (NS+HT, shifts, utility) for replay; plus my policy-free physical lower bound so the card brackets rather than asserts.
- **C3.** Endogenous only; causality and cast-commitment are the mechanisms that close the gap, not a fudge; simulated-vs-measured overheal is a **reported curve with a named residual** from the start, promoted to a pass/fail gate only after commitment and causality are implemented.
- **C4.** Environment in replay, assignment in synthetic, gated: player share >=90% run normally, 70–90% print the free-rider/self-sufficient bracket, <70% validate-only.
- **C5.** Event-driven, agreed, low-stakes; the discipline that matters is zero allocation in the run loop (pooled events, parallel flat arrays), which B costs for storage but not for the search's ~36 MB of per-search garbage.
- **C6.** Review ships as a dashboard tab (B is right on cost and on where the author already is); the window arrives with Setup; the provenance block travels with the numbers either way.
- **C7.** First-class, `minActivity = 0` default, and report both `waitFraction` **and** `max(waitRun)`.
- **C8.** Validation first, B's tolerances (mana 1%/3%, HP 5%/15%), split gate (mana fails all, one target's HP excludes that target), `apiBase`/`apiCasting` per sample, and Coach hard-disabled on: mana failure, any group death, player share <70%, drift >3% on spells >=10% of spend.
- **C9.** Two tiers: full event streams for ~6 fights gated high (>=25s, >=8 casts) with value+recency+pin retention (B); compact summary rows for ~200 fights gated low (>=15s, >=4 casts) for unbiased habits (A). Parallel flat arrays; capture initial auras, form transitions, every cast with cost, HP-at-cast, cooldowns/NS, deaths, absorbs, `GetSpellBonusHealing` samples, per-snapshot maxHP.
- **C10.** B's three additions (`utility`, `prehot`, `shift`) plus my `late` and split `early(Rejuv)`/`stack`; HP-at-cast as evidence on every label; **`unclassified` printed with its mana** and the sum-to-spend identity enforced; habits block shows top three by mana only.
- **C11.** Lexicographic (deaths -> seconds-below-floor -> mana -> robustness -> fewer binds); mana as headline with B's downtime line under it and a "nothing needed to change" verdict; coordinate descent as the only mode with a cancel button; ~10 ms/plan budget; fitted-on-N-1 / held-out-fight reported once >=5 same-zone recordings exist.
- **C12.** Causality invariant in the file (`Plan:Decide` sees no future event), cast locked until it lands, any fight with a death is validate-only, `overkill` correction on killing blows, threat/kill-speed coupling disclosed in the caveat line.
- **C13.** Per-target (not per-role) 5s damage windows, big hits defined as >=15% of that target's maxHP, joint severity sampled by replaying a real fight rather than composing marginal quantiles; provenance (n fights, seconds, zone, date, CI) in the preset file.
- **C14.** Tune usage freely; rank/bind advice only at the moment a new rank is learned (B's gear-toast route), plus the one passive exception where the dashboard's Pareto filter and the sim independently agree — printed in Review, never as a toast.
- **C15.** GCD as a hard 1.5s scheduling constraint separate from cast time; cast commitment (B); reaction 0.5s **only at post-idle decision points**, zero when chaining, refined from recordings with provenance.
- **C16.** (0) HP-at-cast + habit line in the existing summary, one file. (1) mana-half replay against BF-1. (2) full recorder. (3) HP-half replay + gates. (3.5) physical floor + "where the mana went". (4) classifier + card + loop-closure delta line. (5) search. (6) Review tab. (7) synthetic Setup. Ship (0) early, but do not condition (2)–(7) on a two-week behaviour test.

## 4. QUESTIONS ONLY THE AUTHOR CAN ANSWER

1. Is the goal "spend less mana" or "drink less"? If drinking is the real cost, the objective should be seconds of downtime and the card should say "you were fine" whenever the pull budget was never threatened — which is all 28 minutes of BF-1.
2. When heroics/raids arrive, do you want recordings there at all? If yes, decide now whether the recorder filters to assigned targets plus tanks — retrofitting a filter after the format is persisted is worse than choosing wrong today.
3. Would you actually rebind a healing spell rank mid-progression if the tool proved a 15% saving — or is the honest answer no, in which case C14 collapses to "tune usage only" and the search can drop rank as a free parameter?
4. On a fight where the replay fails validation, do you want the curves shown with Coach greyed, or the fight hidden entirely? (Determines whether validation reads as honesty or as breakage.)
5. What is the acceptable wall-clock for a Coach run — 3 s, 10 s, 30 s? At ~10 ms/plan this single number decides whether the search is 300 evaluations or 3,000, and therefore whether the plan family can grow at all.
